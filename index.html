A mindful interruption

Pause.

You don't have to act on every urge.

01 · Create a little space

Take three slow breaths.

Let your shoulders soften. Breathe gently, without forcing or holding your breath.

Breathe in gently
Breath 1 of 3 · Inhale for 4 seconds
02 · Notice what is happening

An urge is not a command.

You can notice the pull to browse without following it.

Urge surfing

Notice the urge. Let it rise and fall without obeying it.

Stay with this thought for a moment.

03 · Return to your intention
↗

One small step is enough.

Bring to mind the task you chose before the urge to browse appeared. Make the next step small and begin there.

A reminder

I can feel an urge and still choose what I do next. I return without judging myself.

Now switch back to your work or close this tab.

No guilt. No judgement. Just a chance to choose again.
